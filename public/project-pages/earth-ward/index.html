Earth Ward

As a part of a research endeavour on site at the Royal Melbourne Children's Hospital a group of designers and I came up with a way to offer patients the healing benefits of nature and parks, but without the pathogens and discomforts that come with it. Keeping them comfortable and able to heal. Our idea was as a new space in the hospital designed for patients, family and staff to all occupy together and bring a new healing mode to the hospital via digital media.

Part of the design is considering how each type of occupant uses the space, and what they each need. Making the space attractive and rid of the sterile and clinical halls of the rest of the hospital. Earth Ward needed to be inviting and a place where patients and family can wander and dwell, this is why the space is broken up into different sections with separate utility. Furniture breaks the symmetry, to make the space feel less clinical. Importantly, the nature that we brought in via digital screens, sounds and projections, is captured from the surrounding parks. The hospital has some beautiful lands just outside, its a pity patients can't go out and experience that. Hence we brought it to the patients instead.
