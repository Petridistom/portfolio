Designing interaction on the web for sharing experiences is a powerful way that I like to create. JavaScript allows me to share something about myself or about the world. It is important for us to be critical of our progress and make sure things are actually better.

As an artform, we can use interactive media to be critical of technology and its marketed use cases. Searching for better ways to use it for the better of our society. In this project I use JavaScript, html, css and p5Play to bring attention to the flaws of AI training models.
