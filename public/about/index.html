ABOUT INTERACTIVE MEDIA VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS UX/UI
I'm Tom and I'm a digital media designer. I like the virtual world, interaction, experiences, stories, 3D, and the web! I have been designing interaction for the web and for virtual environments for over 4 years.
As a designer, I have spent a lot of time working up the skills and the knowledge of many tools, to enable me to create anything. But recently, and after years of effort, reading, asking questions, and receiving mentorship, I've sided with theory as a main practice for design. Asking why?, who is this for?, how will it be used?, how can I encourage a certain behaviour?, is the center of everything I make. So, with that in mind, I'm here to design interactive experiences for the web and for virtual environments that teach the user something about myself or about the world.

Informing my art and design is the life I've lived so far. I've been playing soccer for over 18 years, travelling overseas solo, going out by myself to parks and museums and movies. I play videogames with friends and listen to live radio on NTS! For me, its important to be a part of many communities, large and small. My reality is me moving through communities, learning what I can from others, sharing what I can and fulfilling my social responsibilities within my community, by being a good actor, and being kind to others. I recognise the benefit of hearing as many perspectives as possible as it keeps us from getting stuck in our own worlds. This is what keeps me happy.
